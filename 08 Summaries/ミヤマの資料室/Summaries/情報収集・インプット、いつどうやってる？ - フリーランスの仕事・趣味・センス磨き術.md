> [!tldr]
> A casual sub-channel conversation on [[ミヤマの資料室]] between gadget YouTuber [[ミヤマ]] and her [[美大|art-school]] classmate [[ひさえ]] about how, when, and through which media they gather information. ミヤマ answers a question she gets constantly from viewers by narrating her entire day of [[インプット|input]] — morning manga-release checks, [[Amazon]]/[[楽天|Rakuten]] recommendation rabbit-holes, [[Instagram]], compartmentalised [[YouTube]] viewing at 2x speed, and a strict 9pm digital cut-off. The throughline is two ideas: never lose sight of the *purpose* of input (don't let [[タイパ|time-efficiency]] and [[コスパ|cost-efficiency]] hijack it), and [[アウトプット|output]] — writing your reactions where others can see them — is the strongest form of input.

## なぜこのテーマ — 他人のインプット術が気になる ([00:00])

[[ミヤマ]] opens her sub-channel [[ミヤマの資料室]] — a relaxed program about film, books, and freelance life — alongside [[ひさえ]], a classmate from their [[美大|art-university]] days. The premise is a question ミヤマ fields constantly in her [[YouTube]] comments, [[Instagram]] story DMs, and [[マシュマロ|Marshmallow]] inbox: *where* and *how* do you gather information? Because her main channel reviews [[ガジェット|gadgets]], people especially want to know where she finds gadget information, at what times of day she takes it in, and how much time she spends. Today she tries to answer all of it in one go — while cheerfully predicting they'll wander off-topic.

## どの媒体で情報を集めるか ([02:10])

Asked to map her sources by medium, ミヤマ rattles off a wide spread. Online: [[YouTube]], [[SNS]], and [[Webメディア|web media]]. Offline print: [[雑誌|magazines]], [[本|books]], [[新書|shinsho]], [[漫画|manga]], and [[映画|film]]. And things she physically goes to see — [[本屋|bookstores]], [[家電量販店|electronics retailers]], and [[展覧会|art exhibitions]] at [[美術館|museums]]. The key point is that she has a fairly detailed internal sense of *which medium she reaches for by purpose* — sources aren't interchangeable, each job has its channel.

## ガジェット情報は Amazon・楽天のリコメンドから ([02:52])

The single most-asked question — where does a gadget YouTuber source product info? — gets a disarmingly mundane answer: the [[Amazon]] and [[楽天|Rakuten]] shopping apps. Because she buys an enormous number of gadgets through them, both platforms' recommendation engines become her feed. She follows "frequently bought together" and similar-item suggestions like a [[数珠つなぎ|chain of beads]], hopping between Rakuten and Amazon and searching for ever-more-convenient items. It's almost all done on her phone, on the sofa, and a single recommendation rabbit-hole can vanish an hour.

> [!quote] [[ミヤマ]] ([04:30])
> 1時間とかは割と秒で秒で溶けてく…気づいたら1時間経ってて、リコメンド機能恐ろしいみたいな。
> *(An hour just melts away in seconds — before I notice, an hour's gone. The recommendation feature is terrifying.)*

A telling caveat: her daily **manga new-release check** is *not* done in those apps. Because she buys manga as ebooks (which can't be added to a cart inside the app), she does it in [[Chrome|Chrome]] on the [[楽天Kobo|Rakuten Kobo]] new-releases page instead. She doesn't open Amazon/Rakuten *every* day — but definitely more than most people, though her [[スクリーンタイム|Screen Time]] doesn't show them near the top.

## Instagram で見ているもの ([06:39])

Comparing [[スクリーンタイム|Screen Time]] live on-camera, ミヤマ's top app is [[Instagram]] (~6h that week; ひさえ's was ~4h6m) — she watches [[YouTube]] on an [[iPad]] and at fixed times, so it doesn't dominate. On Instagram her longest-watched genre is [[筋トレ|strength-training]] / [[ボディメイク|bodybuilding]] creators and "what I ate today" food clips from influencers worldwide. Second is her [[推し|oshi]] (favourites) on official and magazine accounts, where she makes a point of liking their appearances. Third is [[ファッション|fashion]] — she actively *searches* terms like [[黒コーデ]], [[モノトーンコーデ]], and [[春コーデ]] for inspiration to feed her own [[OOTD]] posts. Notably, fashion never surfaces in her algorithmic feed precisely because she spends so much more time on bodymaking content — a neat illustration of how the [[アルゴリズム|algorithm]] mirrors watch-time, not stated interest.

## 1日の情報収集ルーティン ([08:41])

The heart of the episode is a beat-by-beat tour of ミヤマ's day, structured around using up [[隙間時間|gap time]]:

- **Morning:** wakes, does ~10 minutes of stretching on the futon, and uses exactly that window for the manga new-release check — if it isn't done in 10 minutes she stops. She's deliberate about not lingering in bed because she's close to a [[睡眠障害|sleep disorder]] and keeps the bed reserved for sleep; once stretching's done, she's *out* no matter what.
- **Breakfast (~30 min):** the same trainee's meal every day while reading manga off an [[iPad]] propped on a tray.
- **Exercise:** during [[筋トレ|strength training]] it's music only (she needs to focus); during the following [[有酸素運動|cardio]] she's allowed video — but with a rule that she must play **10+ minutes of a [[韓国語|Korean]]-learning [[YouTube]] video first**, then spends the remaining ~20 minutes on fellow gadget YouTubers, [[Vlog|vlogs]] (she especially loves women's vlogs), and business media like [[PIVOT]] and [[NewsPicks|ニューズピックス]].
- **Lunch & dinner:** manga again. **Afternoon:** focused work, nothing else.
- **Bath:** manga on an [[iPhone]] (the iPad isn't waterproof); during skincare and hair-drying after every morning/evening shower, [[YouTube]].
- **Night:** manga allowed until 9pm; after 9pm a strict rule — **no digital devices except writing her diary** — so she reads [[紙の本|paper books]], gets naturally sleepy, and is in bed by ~10pm.
- **Out and about:** short transit → a quick [[SNS]] check; longer transit → she always carries a paper book and reads.

> [!quote] [[ミヤマ]] ([13:02])
> 夜9時以降は絶対にデジタルデバイスを開いてはいけない…日記を書く以外の行動はしてはいけないっていう縛りにしていて、大体読書してます。紙の本。
> *(After 9pm I'm absolutely not allowed to open a digital device — no activity but writing my diary — so I mostly read. Paper books.)*

## YouTube 視聴の工夫と倍速 ([13:43])

Despite the long list of channels she follows — most posting 30-minute-plus videos — ミヤマ does watch them to completion, almost always at **2x speed** (3x for slow talkers). [[ひさえ]] can't manage 2x for information content (~1.25x is her limit); both watch [[映画|films]] and [[アニメ|anime]] at normal speed. The standout exception is [[勝間和代|Katsuma Kazuyo]], whom ミヤマ admires and references heavily — too fast to follow at 2x, so she'll watch at 1x.

Viewing is also matched to her **hands and eyes**: hair-drying, skincare, and exercise free her eyes to fully watch the screen, so she saves [[Vlog|vlogs]] and visually-dependent content for then. Chores that demand attention — dish-washing, where running water masks audio — get audio-first content like [[PIVOT]] / [[NewsPicks|ニューズピックス]] played through [[ノイズキャンセリング|noise-cancelling]], plus [[ポッドキャスト|podcasts]] and her oshi's radio. Effectively she's *listening*, not watching.

## 倍速で見るもの・見ないもの、そして『映画を早送りで観る人たち』 ([16:14])

The speed conversation circles a [[職業病|occupational]] root cause. ミヤマ even edits her own vlogs at 2x while editing, which may be why she watches at 2x; [[ひさえ]] edits at normal speed (except short-form, which is built at ~1.5x density and forces 1x editing), and thinks that's bled into her inability to speed-watch. ひさえ raises [[稲田豊史|Inada Toyoshi]]'s book *[[映画を早送りで観る人たち|People Who Watch Movies on Fast-Forward]]*, which ミヤマ recently read and enjoyed. Their shared read: at the cinema, where you've paid, everyone wants normal speed (and can't fast-forward anyway); streaming "for free" invites 2x. The value, they note, ends up attached to the *money paid* rather than the *content itself* — pure [[タイパ|time-efficiency]] / [[コスパ|cost-efficiency]] thinking that quietly loses the [[本質|essence]].

## インプットの本質 — 目的を見失わない ([19:41])

Asked what matters most about input, ミヤマ's answer is immediate: **don't lose sight of the purpose** (目的を見失わないこと). The real point of input is to *create something* or to *gain enjoyment*; the moment the goal becomes "extract maximum quantity for the time/money I spent," it stops being fun. When there's a concrete purpose, hold onto it relentlessly — otherwise [[タイパ|タイパ]] and [[コスパ|コスパ]] take over and you find yourself chasing volume to recoup sunk time on things you didn't even want.

> [!quote] [[ミヤマ]] ([19:45])
> インプット情報収集の大事なことは、目的を見失わないこと。
> *(The important thing about input and information-gathering is not to lose sight of your purpose.)*

## アウトプットこそ最強のインプット ([20:47])

ミヤマ admits [[アウトプット|output]] has become almost *too much* of a goal for her — especially with film, manga, and books, which she consumes purely because she loves them, yet now feels pressure to output since [[YouTube]] made everything she shares tied to making a living. Still, she insists it's a habit worth keeping: re-expressing what you took in, **in your own words**, fixes it in memory and makes the re-enjoyment richer. Concretely she logs nearly every film on [[Filmarks]] (`rebecca_miyama`), curates manga on [[楽天ROOM|Rakuten Room]] (`revyca`) — she reads ~200 a month so she's selective — and writes up **100% of books read**, with reactions, on Rakuten Room (whose generous 500-character limit she's never hit).

> [!quote] [[ミヤマ]] ([22:24])
> インプットしたものを改めて自分の言葉でアウトプットし直すと、読んだものがより脳みそに定着して、より楽しく面白く感じる瞬間がものすごくたくさんある。
> *(When you re-output what you took in, in your own words, what you read settles into your brain far more — and there are so many moments where it feels more fun and more interesting.)*

For the sub-channel this doubles as work: she reads books *for* a topic (e.g. a recent travel video), summarises them in her own words so she can speak about them more broadly on camera, and does it **immediately** after finishing — jotting a memo in [[iPhone]] Notes / [[リマインダー|Reminders]] with a self-nudge to redo it properly on Rakuten Room later if she's pressed. Books especially demand this: their [[情報密度|information density]] is so high that without a written summary the content simply "flies away" (飛んじゃう), however dense it was.

## エンディング ([26:42])
